 Saturday 6th June

Paris Branch member Marc Laurenceau, who is a serving officer in the Régiment d'Infanterie de la Marine based in Draguignan, invites us to join him and his association at Bayeux Commonwealth Cemetery on 6th June, the 71st Anniversary of the landings.  Marc is the founder and Président of the Association DDay-Overlord, which organiizes a guide service to families visiting the graves of US soldiers relations of theirs in Normandy cemeteries.  With the cooperation of the Paris Branch he is seeking to extend this service to Commnwealth families, which means essentially Canadians and British.

We expect to meet up at Bayeux Commonwealth Cemetery at 14h.  You are invited to put this date in your diariy, and details will be circulated a little closer to the event.
