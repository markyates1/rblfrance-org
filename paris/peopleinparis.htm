The Royal British Legion 

Paris Branch 

The Paris branch has some 150 members - and is more than willing to increase that number should you care to join us.

We have members of several nationalities, some have served in the British Armed Forces, some in the French Armed Forces and some who have never served in any armed forces at all. All are welcome.
