THE ROYAL BRITISH LEGION

PARIS BRANCH

NEWSLETTER 
SEPTEMBER  2011

Chairman’s Report

We trust you will have had a pleasant summer break. Borrowing from Keats, here we are again in Autumn, “Season of mists and mellow football boots”. 

Your Committee meets again on 22nd September, and you will have spotted that the first Club Lunch of the new season takes place on the same day. Inspired by their successful 90th Anniversary Dinner-Dance in May Janet Warby and Joan Hicks have drawn up the attached programme of lunches for the remainder of the winter season. They are also planning the traditional Poppy Coffee Morning at the Ambassador’s Residence. As indicated in their flyer attached this will take place on 3rd November.

We are planning another battlefield visit, this time in Normandy, under the guidance of Branch member Colonel Oliver Warman, late Welsh Guards. The tour takes place on 14th-16th October.  You will find the Information Notice attached.

Jointly with the RNVRA we plan to hold a ceremony at the grave of Admiral Sir Bertram Ramsay in Saint Germain-en-Laye Cemetery on Saturday 22nd October. Details will follow. You will recall that Sir Bertram was in charge of the naval operation to bring British and French troops from Dunkirk to England in the Little Ships, and was in 1944 Naval Commander in Chief of the Allied Naval Expeditionary Force in support of the Normandy landings.  A pivotal figure in WWII therefore, who died in a plane accident in Toussus-le-Noble on a mission to meet General Montgomery in Brussels in January 1945.

Our Financial Year ends on 30th September, and this is the time of year when next year’s subscriptions are paid.  Our Membership Secretary Mark Yates has included here his letter to members with the Subscription Renewal Form.  

This gives me the opportunity to thank you all on behalf of our Committee for your continuing commitment to the cause of The Royal British Legion.  We especially appreciate the moral support given us by members who are not necessarily able to participate in our activities for geographical or health reasons. 

Your Committee is planning a range of events in the coming year, and we look forward to seeing you again soon in our Clubhouse.

“We will remember them”
