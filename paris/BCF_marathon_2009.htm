The Royal British Legion 

Paris Branch 

The British Charitable Fund helps British people in France who have fallen on hard times, most of those they help are elderly and many alone.

The Paris Branch of the RBL works closely with the BCF which frequently enables us to almost double the grants we are able to make in cases of hardship amongst ex-military personnel in France.

This is why we would encourage any and all of you to support their entry for the Paris Half Marathon, Dr Gunita Jolly.

Gunita is a young GP working in France and a trustee of the British Charitable Fund in Paris, and she will be running in the Half Marathon on Sunday 8th March in order to raise funds for the British Charitable Fund.

If you would like to sponsor her, please fill in the sponsorship form and send it in, or for more information contact Julia Kett:
