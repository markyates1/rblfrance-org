Remembrance — A Schools Project

Informing students of all ages about remembrance, explaining its importance, and encouraging young people to express remembrance in a meaningful way.

Schools Project

The Project

In 2014 the Royal British Legion launched a high-profile remembrance campaign in the United Kingdom, culminating with the 'Blood Swept Lands and Seas of Red' poppy art feature in the Tower of London. Inspired by that success, the Paris Branch hosted a meeting in early 2015 for teachers who had expressed interest in a working partnership with the RBL, and formulated a Remembrance project aimed at Anglophone schools and sections in Paris and its suburbs.

Participation

Can your school take part? Yes!

“Remembrance” is an invitation to pupils to express their understanding of the theme in whatever way they wish, using one or several media. It is an opportunity for teachers to integrate a theme into their work, encouraging interaction and cooperation between subjects. The project is part of a partnership between schools in the international community, with the emphasis on participation rather than winning. The theme is not restricted to the First World War — it could be inspired by any conflict. There are four categories — KS is Key Stage, the English school system; French equivalents are given in brackets: Primary (école primaire, CP to CM2), KS 3 (collège, 6ème, 5ème and 4ème), KS 4 (3ème at collège and 2nde at lycée), and KS 5 (lycée, 1ère and Terminale). The project should be presented in English but can be cross-curricular.

Visit

We can visit your school

A member of the Royal British Legion can present the concept of “Remembrance”, suggest ways to use the theme to motivate students and create cross-subject projects which will enhance your curriculum, in partnership with the international community in France. Contact: remembrance@rblfrance.org.
