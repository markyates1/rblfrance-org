Welcome

The Royal British Legion in France

The Royal British Legion has been represented in France since 1921, when the Paris Branch was established. Its branches bring together members of different nationalities who support the aims of the Legion through welfare assistance, Remembrance and social activities.

Remembrance

The Legion takes part in commemorative ceremonies throughout France, remembering those who served and those who lost their lives.

History

The Legion in France since 1921

The Paris Branch was established in May 1921, shortly before the formation of the British Legion in the United Kingdom. It has a long history of assisting former service personnel and participating in commemorative events in Paris and elsewhere in France.
