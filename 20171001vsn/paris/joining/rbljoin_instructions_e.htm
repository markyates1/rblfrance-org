However, if you pay Income Tax in France and wish to claim your subscription as a charitable donation, or if you have a French bank account and wish to pay your subscription with a French cheque it is enough to download the following two-page document,

Membership application form

Print it out (on both sides of one sheet if you wish) and fill in the following:

  • Section A,
  • Section B if you have served in the BRITISH armed forces
  • Section G on page 2 (signature and date).

    Before going further, please click here

Send your application, with your cheque for 41 Euros (the annual subscription), to
