Please note that, if you have a UK bank account, and if you pay taxes in the UK. It may be advantageous to you and to the RBL to join online. In that case click on the link here:

http://www.britishlegion.org.uk/members/become-a-member/

However, if you pay Income Tax in France and wish to claim your subscription as a charitable donation, or if you have a French bank account and wish to pay your subscription with a French cheque it is enough to download the following two-page document, print it (recto verso if you wish) and to fill in the following

  • Section A,
  • Section B if you have served in the BRITISH armed forces
  • Section G on page 2 (signature and date).

Send your application, with your cheque for 41 Euros, to

The Secretary,
RBL,
28 rue des Acacias
75017 PARIS.
