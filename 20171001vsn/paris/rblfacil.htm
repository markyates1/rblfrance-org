The Royal British Legion 

Paris Branch 

Members' Facilities :

With some regret, in 2009, the Paris Branch left its premises at 8 rue Boudreau which had housed the activities of the Paris Branch for about 60 years. New legislation on the need for fire exits, access for the handicapped etc. finally made a move inevitable. This has now been accomplished to general satisfaction.

Our address is now 28 rue des Acacias, Paris 17. Our new premises have the legally prescribed facilities and they are proving emminently satisfactory.

Activities include a monthly lunch and you can see the dates and how to reserve this and any other events on the "What's on?" page from the menu on the left.

The premises can be made available, by arrangement, to members and other organisations for private functions and catering facilities are an option.
