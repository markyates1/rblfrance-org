A special appeal :

We have received this appeal from Albert Figg who is struggling to obtain funds to embelish one of the lesser known battlefields of Normandy with a an avenue of trees. The Paris Branch of the RBL feels that this cause is well worthy of your support and would encourage you to help Albert to attain his goal in time for the 70th anniversary of the action.

I have reproduced his letters and illustrations as received and you can see them below. Here is a link to the website which was made when the tank was installed on Hill 112 click here . Albert can be contacted at albertfigg@hotmail.com

Cheques should be made out to: "Hill 112 film and tree fund" and addressed to:
