A word from our Chairman

Since our AGM on 28 November, RBL Paris has had a new Chairman, David Bean, who was elected by a majority of those present.

A new letter will be posted to the site very soon……..

In the meantime for the festive season, David posts the following message:

Dear Fellow Members,

On behalf of your Branch's Officers and the Committee, I would wish you all our seasonal greetings. 
We hope that you will each have a Happy and Peaceful Christmas.
 
Let me add our best wishes for 2017.  
Hopefully it will prove to be a healthy and enjoyable year for us all!

David Bean

Chairman.
