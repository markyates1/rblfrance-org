FOR YOUR DIARY

Saturday, 4th January, 2003 at 3 p.m.
Ceremony at the Cemetery of Pantin, on the N2 in the Centre of Pantin to honour 100 Commonwealth War Graves in Division 6 of the Cemetery, these men having fallen in the Battles of the Aisne and Marne (1914 and 1918).

All welcome.

Friday, 21st February, 2003 at 7.30 for 8 p.m.
Dîner-débat at Paris Branch Clubhouse: 8, rue Boudreau, 75009 Paris.
The Guest Speaker is Monsieur Jacques Zermati, who will talk about the French S.A.S., and his experiences in that unit during the Second World War.

Further details: Phone Social Secretary Joan Hicks 01 30.54.33.06
