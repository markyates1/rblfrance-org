The Royal British Legion 

Paris Branch 

A word from our Chairman

" Who joins our branch and why ?"

"Our membership profile is very interesting and is worth mentioning here. About 30% of our members are French; most of the balance are British, but there are several Dutch, American and Canadian members. Many of our French members served with the Free French during the Second World War. A number of the British members are long-stay expatriates, and in many cases have French spouses. So we have an attractive international blend. Our very successful Chess Section is run by an American member, with many young French players in his teams, who take part in French league championships. A new Golf Section is run by -of course- a Scot. We have a thriving Snooker section, with a regular snooker evening. We plan to launch a Bridge Section. Do you play Chess, Golf, Snooker or Bridge ? About one-third of our membership will have served in the British Armed Forces, either as regulars or national servicemen. But membership of the Paris Branch of the Royal British Legion is open to anyone, of any nationality, who supports the aim of helping former British ex-servicemen and their dependents who have fallen on hard times. People are attracted by the deep satisfaction of contributing to a worthwhile charitable cause, and while sharing these aims, everyone enjoys the comradeship to be found in a British club setting, right in the centre of Paris. The clubhouse is a place to enjoy a quiet lunch or an evening pint of beer, away from from the hurly burly of public places. Who could resist the charms of a British bar manned by a Scottish barman, who on ceremonial occasions will wear the kilt !
