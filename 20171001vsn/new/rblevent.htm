The Royal British Legion 

Paris Branch 

Events

From 1924 onwards, apart from the war years, there has been an Annual Service of Remembrance on November 11th, which is a national holiday in France. This ceremony is organised by the Royal British Legion, as it became in 1964, and the wreath is normally laid either at the foot of the British Memorial Plaque or on the alter steps by the British Ambassador who, during his period of office, is Honorary President of the Paris Branch of the Royal British Legion.
