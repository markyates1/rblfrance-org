The Royal British Legion 

Paris Branch 

Members' Facilities :

  With its premises at 8 rue Boudreau the RBL offers its out-of-town members a useful pied à terre for their visits to Paris. Right in the centre of the city, with very reasonable bar and meal prices and ideally situated to slot in a visit between shops and museum, between the cinema and the bank, it is the perfect place to drop in for lunch.

For those working in the area of the Opera it is a haven of peace where one can get a quiet drink and/or lunch away from the madding crowd.

Numerous event and activities take place at the RBL, notably Snooker, Chess, Bridge, Scrabble, Theatre. There is a "mess night" once a month where members gather for a convivial dinner.

Membership is by no means restricted to the British. The Paris branch has a substantial membership of French (and other) nationalities too. Attracted by the British atmosphere which prevails - and which, in Paris, can be considered almost exotic, many of them consider the Legion bar the ideal context in which to practice their English in a convivial background. (although enough French is spoken to ensure that those who do not speak English will not suffer unduly from hunger or thirst on the premises!)

The RBL is open from 12h00 to 14h30 weekdays and from 18h30 on Club Nights.

Menu of hot dishes available.
