WELCOME to our website

Thank you for playing a part in the historic evolution of the Bordeaux Branch of the Royal British Legion, by visiting our website. Many of our Members enjoy the facility of connecting via the Internet with family and friends perhaps business and pleasure.

We are very pleased to be able to offer the same facility and convenience in respect of RBL Bordeaux Branch news, Members views and information on forthcoming events.

We aim to be modest in our early stages of development in this new media. However with the passage of time and support and contribution from you and other Members we hope to build up greater website content and information services.

Please contact me by email and let me know what you think of progress to date and please give us the benefit of any views you might have on how to improve our website.

Jack Douay M.B.E.
Chairman
Royal British Legion - Bordeaux Branch
14th March 2003
