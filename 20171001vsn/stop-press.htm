6/6/12 - Obit.

ROSEMARY BATEAU CLUBB

Dear Members,
It is with much regret that we report the sudden death of Rosemary Bateau Clubb at the age of 84.
Rosemary joined the Legion five years ago, and said she regretted not having become a member earlier. She enjoyed our monthly lunches and was a keen participant in other RBL events.
We do not know any details of funeral arrangements, but will update you as and when we hear anything.
May She Rest in Peace.
