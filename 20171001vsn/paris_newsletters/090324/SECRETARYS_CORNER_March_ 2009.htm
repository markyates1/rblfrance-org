SECRETARY’S CORNER

 

 

 

Dear Members,

 

We hope that you are all well and looking forward to Spring; this winter has been a long and hard one.

 

You will see some items in this newsletter from RBL London.  The first one is on the make-over of the Legion Website.  Please do have a look if you are on Internet as this makes good reading and it lets us know what is going on around the other branches within the Legion family http://www.britishlegion.org.uk/ .  The second item is an Invitation to World War 2 veterans of Normandy and Arnheim.  This takes the shape of The War and Peace Show at the Hop Farm, Paddock Wood, Kent from 22 – 26 July 2009.   

 

One other item to bring to your attention is that of a proposed trip to the Limousin region of France.  This trip is based on a 3 star hotel.  We would make our own way to the area and meet up at the hotel.  We understand that the price is a bit on the high side and are working on a cheaper type of hotel.

 

If you would like to go on this proposed trip, please contact the Chairman or David Bean.

 

Unfortunately we will not be holding a Club Lunch in April due to the Easter holiday and also not being able to find a suitable venue at a reasonable price.  Our Social Secretary, Joan Hicks, is working very hard to try and find somewhere that is both large enough and reasonable and near either an RER or Metro.

 

Although not much seems to be happening at the moment, we are working very hard trying to come up with events for you to attend if able.  We are in a relative quiet time of the year; I am sure that will all change with our proposed move of headquarters.

 

If you know of anyone who is either ex-service or would like to know more about the Legion, please do not hesitate to contact either myself or Mark Yates, our Membership Secretary.  We can then pass on the information needed.

 

 

 

Janet Warby                                   Joan Hicks                   Mark Yates
