THE ROYAL BRITISH LEGION
PARIS BRANCH

LEGION LETTER

MARCH   2009

 

 

 

Chairman’s Report

This appears on the surface to be a relatively quiet time in the Paris Branch‘s year, but in fact your Committee is working overtime steering our property project.  Here we can now report progress: we have signed the Acte de Vente on our premises at rue Boudreau.  It is now our ambition to sign the Promesse de Vente on the property in rue des Acacias just as soon as possible, and all the relevant paperwork is currently being processed to that end.. Given that work needs to be carried out to these premises to meet our requirements we calculate that we should be in a position to be fully operational there by the Rentrée 2009.   We are considering the appropriate form of celebration of that event.

Apart from the workload on that front, which must be your Committee’s priority,  there is of course an impact on our social activities.  While our buyer has kindly allowed us access to rue Boudreau for our meetings for at least a further two months we are not of course in a position to mount any social events, We are nevertheless hoping to hold one or more lunches in the next month or so, when a suitable venue and tariff can be negotiated. We want to thank Carmen Thourot for so kindly hosting our lunch in February, which was a great success.

So long as we remain in this No-man’s-land for social events we are nevertheless able to draw attention to certain firm fixtures over the next couple of months.  In particular we announce the event on 23rd April when, jointly with our friends from Mémoire et Espoirs de la Résistance, we shall be laying wreaths once more at the statues in Paris of Sir Winston Churchill and Général de Gaulle. This will be followed by a lecture at the Fondation de la Résistance in French by our very own Noreen Riols, whose contribution to our evening at the Ambassador’s Résidence in November was so much appreciated by our French friends.

Another event is planned for June 5th-7th: we wish to thank our neighbours at rue Boudreau, the Maison du Limousin, for proposing a visit to their region, and in particular to the village of  Oradour-sur-Glane, where of course that dreadful massacre took place of women and children at the hands of the S.S. on 10th July 1944.  A information notice is attached on pages 4-5. 

Finally our Committee wish to bring to your attention the Grand National Sweepstake for the race to be held at Aintree on 4th April.  Your tickets are enclosed and your support to this and other fund-raising events is always greatly appreciated by your Treasurer.

 

« We will remember them »

Roger Thorn
