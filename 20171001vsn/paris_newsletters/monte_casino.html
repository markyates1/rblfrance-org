This, our major outing for 2008 should be of great interest to many of our members and we look forward to seeing many of you on this occasion. We especially hope it will be an opportunity to meet some you who live too far from Paris to participate in our usual events. It goes without saying that RBL members from other branches in France will be more than welcome to join us.

Visit to Monte Cassino

Now
Then

This will be a joint project by the Paris Branches of the Oxford Society and the Royal British Legion.

The visit to the battlefields of Salerno and Monte Cassino is planned for Friday 3rd - Sunday 5th October 2008.

Our highly competent guide, whom some of you will know from our other visits to nearer theatres of war, will be Col. Oliver Warman (Balliol) late Welsh Guards.

The programme proposed is as follows:

Friday 3rd:

Main party fly Easyjet Paris-Naples arriving lunch-time.
They will be met by the tour coach who will leave for the first part of the tour, taking in Salerno.
That evening will be spent in a hotel at the coastal resort of Gaeta. . .

Saturday 4th:

The tour continues working towards Monte Cassino. The coach returns for a second night at Gaeta. .

Sunday 5th:

The tour continues over the battlefields, and the coach will return to Naples airport in the late afternoon.
The party return to Paris by Easyjet flight.

COST:

It is estimated that the tour will cost approximately 400 euros per head.
This will include the flights Paris - Naples return; 2 nights in the hotel and the coach hire for 48 hours.

Those interested in taking part in this tour should contact: Roger Thorn (roger.thorn@wanadoo.fr) as soon as possible
so that a confirmed booking can be made of the hotel rooms.
