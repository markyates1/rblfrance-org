Would your school like to participate?

 

"Remembrance"; is an invitation to pupils to express their understanding of the theme in whatever way they wish, using one or several media. 

The project is part of a partnership between schools in the International community with the emphasis on participation rather than winning. 

  • The theme is not restricted to the First World War. It could be inspired by any conflict.

  • There are four categories
    • Primary
    • KS 3 (6/5/4 ème)
    • KS 4 (3ème/2nde)
    • KS 5 (1ère /Terminale)

  • The project should be presented in English but can be cross curricular and take any form – written, art, oral, film etc. Pupils and students should be able to speak about their entry and explain the reasons behind their choices. 

  • The R.B.L. will support schools as requested, during the project

  • In the event of multilple entries in the same category from the same school the R.B.L. may select those which will go forward to a display and/or event celebrating the achievements of all participants 

  • Entries can be individual or group

  • The maximum size for an entry is A1 (approx 60cm x 84cm) 

  • In conjunction with the project we hope to create a dedicated website with secure access for all schools where students can post and share their achievements.

  • The closing date for entries is the February holidays. The display and an event will take place during the summer term.

  • If you would like to join the schools already participating in the Remembrance Project please email remembrance@rblfrance.org

We can visit your school

A member of the Royal British Legion can present the concept of "Remembrance", suggest ways to use the theme to motivate students and create cross-subject projects which will enhance your curriculum in a partnership with the International community in France.

Contact Janet at remembrance@rblfrance.org for further details as to how this can be arranged.

The Remembrance Tree,


Designed and created by students from Lycée Pasteur, Neuilly sur Seine.


The Lycée won a special prize for a whole class entry.

 

 

‘Remembrance’ was an invitation to pupils to express their understanding of the theme in whatever way they wished, using one or several media.

 

 

Almost one hundred and sixty entries from pupils, ranging in age from ten to seventeen, were received for this year’s competition. 

Contributions ranged from poetry to a piano composition, artwork to film, and a virtual book to an imaginative ‘Remembrance tree’.

Senior students from the participating Lycées, both native and non-native English speakers, particularly  impressed the judges by their eloquence and passion when speaking about their remembrance work.
