The Chess Section :

  CHESS:

The Chess section of the RBL Paris Branch is participating in the Telethon (which supports research into cures for myopathies) on Sat. 6th December, between 14:00 and 19:00 hrs.

Our Chess section organizers are proposing:

1) A first-ever Chess 960 simultaneous exhibition on 15 boards twice, by our section's master, Guillermo Scholz. Minimum entry fees of €5 per game. Winners will receive chess books.

2) An introductory course in Chinese Chess (Xiang-Qi) by our top player, Marc Nguyen, for minimum of €3 per learner. Each learner will receive a chess set and beginner's book.

3) An introductory course in Japanese Chess (Shogi) by our 2007 Pdt, Luc Escoffier, for a minimum of €3 per learner. Each learner will receive a beginner's book.

All entry fees will go to to the Myopathy Research Association, a truly noble cause.

Seven of our chess section members are devoting their time and energy to preparing this event for you at the RBL Paris branch. All are welcome to come and enjoy Britain's most popular mind-sport. For more info : phone 01 43 87 69 96 (leave a message on the answering machine if necessary).
