Report of past event

Naujac sur Mer - 9th May 2009

On Saturday 9th May a memorial was unveiled at Naujac-sur-Mer in the  Médoc to commemorate 7 airmen who crashed in their Short Stirling on 12th March 1944 whilst mine-laying in the Gironde estuary.

At the crash site near the coast wreaths were laid by relatives of the aircrew as well as the British Ambassador Sir Peter Westmacott, the RBL, RAFA, RNA, UNC and municipal organisations including children from the village. The RBL Chairman John Allsop attended with Standard Bearer Tom Lewis and other members and their wives. The Legion standard was paraded together with some twenty British, Commonwealth and French standards.
