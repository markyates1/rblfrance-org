Report of past event

Remembrance Sunday - 9th November 2008

The annual Remembrance Day ceremony took place on a beautiful sunny day on Sunday, 9th November at the Allied Military Cemetery in Bordeaux Talence. The service, in the new Remembrance Day format, was taken by Paul Dixon, deputising for the new Chaplain who has not yet arrived in France. Wreaths were laid by HM Consul, Alastair Roberts, John Allsop for the RBL, Capt. Chris Barnacle for the RNA, Beryl Dennet for RAFA, Mike Pratt for the Bordeaux British Community (BBC) and the Mayor Adjoint of Talence.

Following the wreath laying Remembrance Crosses were placed on the Commonwealth and US graves. The RBL Chairman gave a short address explaining the significance of the poppy to the assembly which numbered about eighty people, more French than British. The standard was ably carried by Gordon Merrett in the absence of Tom Lewis, Richard Terol sounded Last Post and Reveille, and Jack Harrison played the pipes.
