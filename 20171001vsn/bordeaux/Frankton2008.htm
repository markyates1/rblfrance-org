The Royal British Legion 

Bordeaux & S.W. France Branch

Recent event:

Operation Frankton 12 December 2008

 

 

The meteo said it would be fine in the morning, but it was not to be. The ceremony at Chateau Magnol to commemorate the deaths of Sgt Wallace and Marine Ewart took place in a steady drizzle, but it did not dampen the spirits of those attending. Veterans from the Frankton Association, the Royal Marines' Association and The Special Boat Service laid wreaths, along with the Maire Adjoint of Blanquefort, the Royal Naval Attache from the British Embassy in Paris and John Murphy on behalf of the RBL. A childrens' choir from Blanquefort College, wearing pictures of the two marines, sang a memorial song, following which the naval Attache and Deputy Mayor gave short addresses. Branch members present included Jack Douay, John Mercer, Ken Napier, Richard Holworthy, Tom Lewis, thankfully restored to good health, with the standard and John Murphy. Angela Larmuth attended in place of her husband John, who was recovering from a minor operation. The ceremony was followed by a vin d'honneur in the Leisure Centre.

The skies cleared by late morning and the afternoon parade on the quais was bright and sunny. It was due to start at 1530, to allow those who lunched at Blanquefort to get there in good time, but the Deputy, who was the Guest of Honour, was delayed by 45 minutes. There were 2 guards of Honour, one from the French Navy and one from the Army, and a very good turnout of local French and British supporters. It is a pity that the bronze memorial is now embedded in the paving stones on the ground where it is not immediately apparant, but when it was mounted on a plinth by hangar 14 it was vandalised.
