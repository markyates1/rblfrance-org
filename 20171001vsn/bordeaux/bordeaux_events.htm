Saturday 11th June          Great Poppy Fête 

The year 2011 is a very important one for the RBL as a whole, as we will celebrate our 90th Anniversary. Many events are being planned already throughout the UK, including concerts in every Cathedral in the country. 

Our Branch celebrations in S.W.France have now been finalised, and a copy of the flyer/poster containing details may be obtained from Chairman John Allsop We would like as many members and friends as possible to join in the fun, as shown at the foot of the flyer. You can also download the flyer by clicking here.

More information is available on www.thegreatpoppypartyweekend.org.uk.

If you would like any of the free 90th Anniversary materials, go to www.britishlegion.org.uk/about-us/publications, or you may order a Poppy Party Pack from www.poppyshop.org.uk.

 

 Thursday 7th to Sunday 10th July    St Girons 2011

The annual four- day hike of the Freedom Trail over the Pyrenees will start from St. Girons, Ariège. Details will follow.
