The Royal British Legion 

Bordeaux & S.W. France Branch

Report of past event

Remembrance Sunday - 11th November 2007

Our annual parade and service took place at the Allied Cemetery, Talence on the 11th November.

As the date coincided with the French ceremony we had a larger than usual turnout. The service followed the new format and was taken by Rev Michael Selman.

Wreaths were laid by HM Consul Alastair Roberts, the Mayor, Ken Napier for the RNA, Beryl Stannard for the RAFA, the Chairman for the Branch, Adrian Revill for the Bordeaux British Community and individual crosses were placed on each of the graves by HM Consul and the Chairman.

Jack Harrison once again led the parade with his bagpipes and played a lament during the wreathlaying ceremony. Tom Lewis was our standard bearer, and it was good to see the RAFA standard on parade again.

After the service we processed to the main Talence cemetery for the French ceremony, and then 17 of us met up at the Molly Malone Restaurant on the quais for an excellent fish and chip lunch.

 
The photos are of Beryl Stannard and members, Tom Lewis and John Allsop, HM Consul, Mayor and Chairman, The Chairmen of the Bordeaux British Community, the Branch, RNA and RAFA.
