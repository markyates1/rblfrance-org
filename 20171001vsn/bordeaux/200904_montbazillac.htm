Report of past event

Visit to Château Montbazillac - Wed 25 May 2009

The Château

We all arrived from different directions, some having travelled further than others to the great Château of Monbazillac to be met by our very informative and also very humorous young lady guide.

The tour was fascinating and we all came away having learned something and I am sure I overheard people say it would be high on their list to take visitors.

The Grand Salon du château

Returning from the château

We were back where we started too soon; time passes quickly when you are having fun!

With some taking the scenic route we arrived at the restaurant, La Tour des Vents, in good time for lunch, and what a lunch! Everyone enjoyed the quality and the presentation.

Handbrake off and just roll down the hill and we were at the Cave Co-operative. A huge selection of fine wines and other goods awaited us with an opportunity to taste and buy. Thanks to Chris Allsop we even had dessert which complemented the sweet Monbazillac wines beautifully.

I think we can safely say that our first social event of the year was a success and we look forward to the next one, provisionally booked for somewhere in the Dordogne on Wednesday 24 June.

Gary Lewis, Social Secretary
