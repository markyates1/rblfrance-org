Report of past event

ST PIERRE D'IRUBE 30APRIL 2005

The yearly ceremony to commemorate the dead of the British and French forces killed in the early hours of 14 April 1814 during the French sortie from the Bayonne Citadel took place in warm sunshine on Saturday April 30th.

Wreaths were laid at the Monument d'Aigle by ex Honourary Consul Robert Hope on behalf of HMCG and the Chairman for the RBL.

 

 

 

This was followed by further wreath laying at the Coldstream Guards Cemetery and the 3rd Guards Cemetery.

The standard was paraded by Tom Lewis, who attended with his wife, Wendy.
