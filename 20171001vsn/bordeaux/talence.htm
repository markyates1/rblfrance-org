TALENCE: Remembrance Day Service

The annual Remembrance Day service took place in sunny weather in the Allied Military Cemetery, Talence on Sunday 9th November at 1030 am.

The service was taken by the Reverend Michael Selman and wreaths were laid by HM Consul General Tom Kennedy, the Mayor of Talence, the President of the Bordeaux British Community, the Royal Naval Association, the Royal Air Forces Association, the Franco British Association and your Chairman. During the service remembrance crosses were placed on the graves by Tom Kennedy and the Chairman, following which the Chairman gave his first ever address in French which he was told was understood. The French armed forces were represented by General Bastien, Commandant la Région Aérienne Atlantique. The standard bearers were led into the cemetery by our piper, Jack Harrison, who played a lament during the wreath laying. The branch standard was paraded by Ken Napier.

Following the parade the standard bearers were inspected by HM Consul, the mayor and the Chairman. After the ceremony some 18 members and friends sat down to a moroccan lunch at the Jeu de Paume restaurant in Mérignac.

On the 11th November the chairman, accompanied by his daughter, represented the branch at the French ceremony which commenced in the Allied military ceremony where a minute's silence was observed at 11 am, and continued at the French war memorial in the main Talence cemetery.
