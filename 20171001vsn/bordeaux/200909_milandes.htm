Visit to Château des Milandes

24th June 2009

The visit to the Château des Milandes proved an interesting venue. Set in magnificent surroundings with views of undulating countryside, the Dordogne river is just adjacent to the château. The tour started with the falconry demonstration which was both informative and skilful. A variety of birds were displayed, with a brief introduction given by the handler on each type, followed by individual flights round the area.

After the show we adjourned with our dedicated guide for a tour of the château originally built around 1489 by the Caumont family and later abandoned during the French Revolution. It was subsequently restored in the 19th century, with a variety of new owners each leaving their marks, the most notable being Joséphine Baker. The rooms house many of her costumes, photographs, playbills and other memorabilia covering her adult life with her many adopted children. Sadly she was forced to vacate her home in 1964 due to financial problems, but some of her wards still visit the château to this day.

We then adjourned for lunch at “Les Relais des 5 Châteaux”. The short drive opened up to magnificent views of at least five châteaux and the crossing of the Dordogne at Castelnaud. The three-course lunch including wine and coffee in the pleasant dining room with excellent views of the surrounding countryside completed an interesting and enjoyable social occasion.

                                                                                                Michael Ramsey
