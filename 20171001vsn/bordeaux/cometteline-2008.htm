The Comete Escape Line Event

Ceremony of 7-9 September 2008,

Le Pays Basque
The song of the Resistance in full flow

Ciboure Cemetery
This event celebrates the second of the WW2 escape lines, the Comete Line, which was founded at the beginning of the war to help allied aircrew and other refugees escape from Belgium and northern France via Anglet, near Bayonne, St Jean de Luz and Ciboure. This year 6 of us, Jack and Janine Douay, Scott and Judy Goodall, John Allsop and myself made the trip to the Pays Basque for the ceremonies in Anglet, Ciboure and Urrunge. Tom Lewis could not attend as he was recovering from an operation, so John carried the standard. The ELMS element did take part from UK so the British participants were back to their normal strength.
The wreathlaying ceremony at Anglet took place in sunshine, but after the vin d'honneur it poured down, causing local flooding. The Saturday morning ceremony at Ciboure cemetery was held in a rainstorm, and Michou, a young 19 years old guide who survived Ravensbruck,and an ex RAF evader, laid the wreaths on the graves of her fellow guides. Both days of the march were cold and wet, but this did not dampen the participants' enthusiasm.
