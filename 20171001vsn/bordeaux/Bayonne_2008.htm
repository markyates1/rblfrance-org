The Royal British Legion 

Bordeaux & S.W. France Branch

Report of past event

 

 

BAYONNE 14 April 2008

Ken Napier reports:

The Bayonne event this year only had 8 standards plus one other, an Armenian one. Why an Armenian flag? Good question, well asked. There was no obvious reason. There were about a dozen watchers, including the Mayor's representative. Sue Napier laid our RBL wreath at the Monument des Aigles. I had our RNA Standard. It was all very low key compared with some previous years.

I looked at two of the French standards. One was from the Para school at Pau and the other a strange one from Verdun in WW1 !

 

 

 

At the 2 British cemeteries I explained a little bit about the 3rd Guards camp, and that Gen Hay, killed there in the battle, was a Royal Scot. The RN had played a major part in recce-ing and getting Wellington's pontoon bridge over the mouth of the Adour, with a fair number of men lost. The weather was awful and the waves over the bar severe.

There was an evening event in Bayonne with drinks and a talk on Napoleon's visit there which we did not attend.

The event does seem to be fading away but 2014 will be the 200th anniversary.
