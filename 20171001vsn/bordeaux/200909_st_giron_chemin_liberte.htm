The Royal British Legion 

Bordeaux & S.W. France Branch

Report of past event

Le Chemin de la Liberté, St Girons, Ariège

8th-11th July 2009

This year’s trek, the 16th since it began, took place in fine weather and was attended by some 40 walkers plus local Branch Chairman John Allsop and wife Christine, Standard-Bearer Tom Lewis plus some 12 local RBL branch members and wives as well as others from UK branches and many French supporters. The usual ceremonies were observed, at the memorials of Kercabanac, the Chemin de la Liberté museum in St.Girons, the barn where Louis Barrau was murdered on the Col de l’Artigue, and at Aunac.

 

 

There were some fine achievements by participants from the UK, including those among the 15 places allocated by long-time organiser Scott Goodall to RBL supporters who raise money for the Poppy Appeal. Two of the British RBL team, Charles Wilson from the Isle of Man and Dennis Frain from Kent each raised an impressive £5000 in sponsorships, and other Legion participants raised at least £1000 each.
