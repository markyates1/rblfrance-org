Recent event:

Operation Frankton 9-10 December 2010

FRANKTON SOUVENIR

The annual ceremonies commemorating Operation Frankton (“The Cockleshell Heroes”)  took place at Blanquefort and Bordeaux on Friday 10th December and in the Médoc on Thursday the 9th. A strong Royal Marine contingent, mainly from the SBS Association and led by Major Malcolm Cavan OBE, RM., was present.   The RBL Branch standard joined a good turnout of standards representing various French ex-service Associations. 

The Blanquefort ceremony was held at the Château Magnol as usual.  This takes place at the site of the bunker beside the German Naval Headquarters where Sgt Wallace and Mne Ewart are believed to have been executed by a firing squad on 12th December 1942. .The ceremony was very well attended despite the early hour and the extreme cold.  After wreaths were laid by various dignitaries, both civil and military, including your Chairman, followed by several speeches, pupils from a local school read and sang several moving pieces.  Once more, one was struck by the emphasis placed by the French on insuring that today’s youth actively participates in these commemorations of the sacrifices made by previous generations.  The ceremony was followed by a vin d’honneur and a collation in the local Town Hall.

In the afternoon a further ceremony took place on the Bordeaux quayside named Quai Frankton after the Operation, where wreaths were laid by the Préfet and other French and British senior civil and military dignitaries.
