The Comète Escape Line, Pays Basque

 

The Comète Escape Line event of 10th-12th September 2010
 
This event commemorates the second of the WW2 escape lines which was founded by Countess Andrée de Jongh (better known as Dédée) at the beginning of the war to help allied aircrew and other refugees to escape to Spain from Belgium and Northern France via Anglet, St Jean de Luz, and Ciboure.
This year a group of Members and friends, including Branch President Jack Douay, and Standard-Bearer Tom Lewis were involved in the various ceremonial aspects of the event, starting with a ceremony at the Monument aux Morts in Anglet which was attended by a large crowd of people including over 20 Anciens Combattants’ standards, followed by a mayoral reception and supper.


On a fine Saturday morning the walkers, numbering almost 100, gathered at the Cemetery in Ciboure where wreaths were laid at the graves of two famous passeurs, Florentino Goicoechea, by his 92-year-old only surviving brother, and Kattalin Aguirre.

 After a hearty breakfast they set off on their way to Urrugne where they were met by the non-walkers, for another wreath-laying ceremony by the RBL and other évadeurs including George Duffee representing the ELMS association, and a buffet campagnard to see them on their way.
Thereafter they continued into Spain by way of the “last house” and the Bidassoa river, ending at Renteria on Sunday.

John Allsop September 2010
