Eden Camp 2006

Rocks Away

Member Scott Goodall and his wife, Judy, together with 15 members of the St Girons Chemin Association recently attended the unveiling of the Helper Memorial at the Eden Camp Museum, York. This was the culmination of months of work involving the transfer a large rock from a St Girons quarry to Yorkshire. The following account is from The Gazette and Herald who have kindly given us permission to reprint it.

Ryedale news Behind the Lines By Gazette Reporter

Lord Crathorne after unveiling the Escape Line Memorial Society's Helper Memorial at Eden Camp

Unsung heroes who helped allied servicemen to escape from Nazi-occupied Europe in the Second World War have been honoured in a ground-breaking ceremony at a Ryedale museum.

On Saturday, the largest gathering of escapees and evaders, together with many ordinary European citizens who helped them to escape, were back behind barbed wire in a former prisoner of war camp Eden Camp, near Malton.

The Lord Lieutenant of North Yorkshire, Lord Crathorne, unveiled the Escape Line Memorial Society's Helper Memorial. Consisting of two large rocks, one from the Pyrenees and the other from the Lake District, the helper memorial weighs more than 11 tons and symbolises the union between the French and the British.

Members of the Resistance from France, Holland, Belgium, Italy, Spain, the Basque country and Scandinavia were at the ceremony. Museum director Nick Hill said: "We were honoured that so many Resistance members attended. While they were only civilians, the helpers faced torture, summary execution or incarceration in concentration camps if they were caught harbouring or assisting any allied servicemen."

Roger Stanton, secretary of the Escape Lines Memorial Society, said: "It was a truly international affair, attended by ambassadorial representatives from many of the former occupied countries, plus all the highly decorated veterans. "The magnificent memorial honours all those helpers who silently bore so much and suffered in the cause of freedom. Sadly, this is probably going to be the last really large gathering of so many from the Escape Lines community."
