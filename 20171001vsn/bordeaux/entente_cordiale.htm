Entente Cordial Ceremonies

Royal Visit Reception 6th April Paris
The Chairman, committee members Jack Douay and Paddy Atkinson and the Reverend Michael Selman were among the 600 guests invited to meet Her Majesty the Queen, our Royal Patron, and the Duke of Edinburgh at the Louvre Museum on the 6th April. It was a memorable, happy and relaxed occasion, once we had passed the very tight security, and we all had a chance to speak to one or other of the royal couple. It was also a good opportunity to meet members of the RBL from other French branches.

Entente Cordiale Ceremony Abjat sur Bandiat.
The Chairman, accompanied by his wife, daughter and branch standard, ventured to the far north of the Dordogne on 8th April, the 100th anniversary of the actual signing of the Entente Cordiale, to join Sue and Stewart Edwards, owners of the Entente Cordiale hotel and restaurant in Abjat sur Bandiat, who had organised a celebration to commemorate the event. The official guests included HM Consul General, Tom Kennedy. The proceedings started in cold sunshine at the war memorial where wreaths were laid by Tom Kennedy, the mayor and the Chairman, who had to reply, luckily in English, to the mayor's speech of welcome. This was followed by a vin d'honneur at the mairie, where we had a chance to meet up with the local dignitaries and the anciens combattants. We then repaired to the hotel where Sue and Stewart had laid on an excellent "best of" repas of French and English dishes. It was a very cheerful event, showing the the true spirit of the friendship existing between the incoming British and their French hosts in the Dordogne. Stewart has been a stalwart supporter of the poppy appeal and it was a pleasure to meet up with him.
