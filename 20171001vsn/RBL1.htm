The Royal British Legion

Paris Branch

 

 

The Royal British Legion Paris Branch has the distinction of being the very first Branch of the organisation, its articles actually pre-dating the official establishment of the RBL. Unfortunately the circumstances of this curious fact are lost in the mists of time - many papers having been lost between 1939 and 1945 - but we know that it was based on an officers' club which was formed very shortly after the Great War.

The organisation was fortunate enough to acquire its premises right in the centre of the city at a time when such a thing was still plausible for a charitable organisation and members thus benefit from use of a highly convenient pied à terre, easily accesible from all points of the city where a quiet drink or a leisurely lunch can be enjoyed in surroundings very different from the overcrowded restaurants and bars in the area.

Although the charitable aims of the organisation are identical to those of any other branch in the world - to raise funds for those who have served in the British armed forces and who now fnd themselves in financial distress, the Paris branch has many members of French (and other) nationalities. These members enjoy the British atmosphere which prevails - and which, in Paris, can be considered almost exotic. Certainly it provides an opportunity for those who wish to practice their English a convivial background in which to do so - although enough French is spoken to ensure that those who don't speak English will not die of hunger or thirst.
