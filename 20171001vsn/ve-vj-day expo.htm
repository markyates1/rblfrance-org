 Dear Member,

VE/VJ DAY COMMEMORATION 60TH ANNIVERSARY EXHIBITION 29/30 September 2005 at THE MILLENIUM ROOM, BRITISH EMBASSY 35 RUE FAUBOURG ST. HONORE, PARIS 8eme

The RBL Paris Branch are organising an Exhibition recalling the events of World War II during 1939/45 which led up to VE and VJ Days. We hope you and your friends will come and visit the exhibition.

The dates are as follows : Wednesday 28th September, Vernissage, 18.30 hrs - 21.00 hrs This will include a Cocktail, for which there will be a "participation".

Thursday 29th September, 14.00 hrs - 18.00 hrs
Friday 30th September, 14.00 hrs - 18.00 hrs

On Thursday and Friday there will be an entrance fee of 10 euros for adults and 5 euros for under 18's.

We would be interested to have your accounts of the war years. You may have been on active service in Britain, France, Africa, Middle East or the Far East. You may have been a child who was evacuated to the country or seaside, a Mother whose children were taken away to safety or yourself evacuated because you were pregnant. You may have been in one of the many Civilian Occupations. You may have been one of those who cheered the King & Queen when they appeared on the Balcony at Buckingham Palace with the Princesses and the Prime Minister, Winston Churchill.
Don't worry if you cannot type, just send your stories and our Secretary will type them up. If you have any photographs or old documents we would love to see them too.

Let's make this an exhibition to remember and inform the youngsters of today what it was like to be young in 1939/45.

If you would like to attend this exhibition please fill in the reply slip and send to Janet Warby, Hon. Secretary, 8 rue Boudreau, 75009 Paris.
