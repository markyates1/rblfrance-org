Royal British Legion Paris Branch

  

 

Our thoughts and prayers go with our thanks for the life of our Monarch, Queen Elizabeth. For her patronage of the Royal British Legion and for her care and concern for all veterans and members of the Armed Forces. In her faith and dedication to duty, she led us to understand the role of duty and care for each other. As our Nation and our Commonwealth mourn her death, may her example continue to inspire us.
