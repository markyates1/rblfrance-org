The Royal British Legion 

Nice-Monaco Branch BR 3473 

Information


Adresse du Siège : 11 Rue de la Buffa, 06000, NICE

 

One of the oldest branches of the RBL in France, the Declaration of Formation of the Nice-Monaco Branch was issued by the Prefecture in Nice on 15th May 1929.

 

The current committee, since year 2020, comprises:

  • Chairman : Commander Micheal Healy, RN, M.B.E.
  • Vice Chairman : Thomas P. Tennant
  • Secretary: Wilhelmina Van Tekelenburg
  • Treasurer: Stephen J. Bull
  • Poppy Appeal Organiser: Elaine Baverstock
Royal British Legion - Nice Monaco - Branch activities consist of the annual Poppy Appeal, and representation at ceremonial events along the Côte d'Azur.
